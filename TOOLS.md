# B2B tool directory

**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-24

This is a small, opinionated directory—not a complete market map. No placement is paid. Choose a tool only after the operating problem, system of record, data boundary, owner, and success measure are clear.

Pricing, packaging, integrations, and product behavior change. Confirm them on the official site before purchasing. Review privacy, security, data rights, deliverability, and applicable laws with qualified owners.

## CRM and system of record

### [HubSpot](https://www.hubspot.com/products/crm)

- **Use when:** a team wants a broadly adopted CRM that can connect sales, marketing, service, and operations.
- **Why it is on the list:** contact, company, deal, activity, pipeline, and reporting workflows can start simply and expand on one platform.
- **Avoid or validate when:** the team has not modeled lifecycle stages, property ownership, permissions, or the cost of adding hubs and seats.

### [Attio](https://attio.com/)

- **Use when:** a modern GTM team needs flexible objects, attributes, views, automations, and a customizable data model.
- **Why it is on the list:** it is useful when the sales process does not fit a rigid default CRM schema.
- **Avoid or validate when:** flexibility would let every team invent a different definition of account, opportunity, or stage.

## Prospect research and enrichment

### [Apollo](https://www.apollo.io/product/prospect-and-enrich)

- **Use when:** a team needs B2B company/contact discovery, filtering, enrichment, and sales-engagement workflows in one place.
- **Why it is on the list:** it can reduce the number of separate systems needed for list building and basic outbound execution.
- **Avoid or validate when:** regional data rights, contact accuracy, source transparency, or niche-market coverage is central to the motion.

### [Clay](https://www.clay.com/use-cases/data-enrichment)

- **Use when:** a GTM engineering or operations team needs multi-provider enrichment, scoring, routing, and trigger-driven workflows.
- **Why it is on the list:** its enrichment and workflow model supports experiments that are difficult to express in a single database search.
- **Avoid or validate when:** the team lacks an owner for data logic, credit consumption, quality sampling, and write-back rules.

## Outbound execution

### [Instantly](https://instantly.ai/outreach)

- **Use when:** cold-email infrastructure, sequencing, inbox management, and deliverability operations are already part of a qualified outbound motion.
- **Why it is on the list:** it brings sending accounts, campaigns, follow-ups, and replies into one operating surface.
- **Avoid or validate when:** the team is using sending volume to compensate for weak ICP evidence, generic copy, or uncertain compliance.

### [Salesforge](https://help.salesforge.ai/en/articles/10333634-how-the-forge-stack-works-end-to-end)

- **Use when:** a team wants email and LinkedIn steps, conditional sequences, reply management, and optional prospecting or mailbox infrastructure in one ecosystem.
- **Why it is on the list:** it supports multichannel execution and can connect to common CRMs and GTM tools.
- **Avoid or validate when:** the team has not separated data sourcing, sending infrastructure, deliverability, sequencing, and CRM ownership.

## Workflow automation

### [n8n](https://n8n.io/features/)

- **Use when:** a technical operator wants visual workflows, APIs, webhooks, custom code, AI nodes, or a self-hosting option.
- **Why it is on the list:** it allows more control over logic and deployment than a purely no-code automation layer.
- **Avoid or validate when:** nobody owns credentials, retries, monitoring, upgrades, and failure recovery.

### [Zapier](https://help.zapier.com/hc/en-us/articles/37518970271245-What-is-Zapier)

- **Use when:** a non-technical team needs a fast trigger-and-action workflow across common business applications.
- **Why it is on the list:** its integration coverage makes many straightforward automations quick to deploy.
- **Avoid or validate when:** task-based cost, branching complexity, data residency, or silent workflow failures could become material.

## Selection checklist

- [ ] The operating problem is written without naming a vendor.
- [ ] One system of record is explicit.
- [ ] Required inputs, outputs, owners, and failure paths are mapped.
- [ ] Data rights, privacy, security, and compliance have qualified reviewers.
- [ ] A small pilot has an observable success condition and stop condition.
- [ ] Total cost includes seats, usage, credits, implementation, maintenance, and migration.
- [ ] The team can export its data and unwind the workflow.

Submit additions only when they solve a distinct job and can be explained with an evidence boundary. A longer list is not automatically a better directory.
