# AI-native B2B GTM tool directory

> A curated directory of modern software for researching markets, creating demand, reaching buyers, operating revenue workflows, and measuring results.

**Directory release:** 33 tools · 13 operating categories

**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-24

This is not a complete market map or a paid ranking. It is a deliberately small, modern-by-default shortlist. A newer product is not automatically better: every entry must solve a defined B2B job, expose a meaningful limitation, and have a current official product or documentation page.

No placement is paid. Lensmor is built and maintained by Ivan Xu, the owner of this repository; its relationship is disclosed in the entry below. All other tools are included independently.

Third-party links include `ref=b2b-playbook` only so destination sites can identify this repository as the referral source. The parameter is not an affiliate code, does not affect inclusion, and does not change the price you pay.

## How this directory uses “AI-native”

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **AI-native** | AI works inside the core product context and can research, decide, generate, or take bounded action. It is more than a detached chat box. |
| **Modern infrastructure** | The product is a strong substrate for an AI-native GTM stack through data, APIs, workflows, integrations, or focused modern UX; AI is not its only value. |
| **Established benchmark** | A mature incumbent retained for ecosystem coverage or comparison. Each category should contain no more than one unless a specific decision requires it. |

An AI-native claim should be supported by observable product behavior: access to relevant business context, multi-step work, tool use or workflow execution, human review or permissions, and a usable output. “AI-powered” copy alone is not enough.

## 01 · CRM, customer data & revenue systems

### [Attio](https://attio.com/platform/ai?ref=b2b-playbook)

**Type:** AI-native · **Reviewed:** 2026-08-24

- **Use when:** a GTM team wants a flexible CRM data model with AI research, classification, summaries, workflows, call context, and agent access close to the records.
- **Why it stands out:** objects, lists, workflows, AI attributes, embedded intelligence, and MCP make the CRM an active operating layer instead of a passive database.
- **Avoid or validate when:** complex permissions, reporting, migration, integration depth, or large-scale CRM administration matters more than flexibility.

### [Day AI](https://day.ai/?ref=b2b-playbook)

**Type:** AI-native · **Reviewed:** 2026-08-24

- **Use when:** a team wants calls, email, meetings, and relationship context to maintain the CRM and surface next actions with less manual data entry.
- **Why it stands out:** its product model starts with customer memory and conversation context, then uses AI to extract structured intelligence with sources and reasoning.
- **Avoid or validate when:** a younger platform must replace a deeply customized CRM; test integrations, field controls, permissions, export, and migration on real workflows.

### [HubSpot](https://www.hubspot.com/products/artificial-intelligence?product=crm&ref=b2b-playbook)

**Type:** Established benchmark · **Reviewed:** 2026-08-24

- **Use when:** a team values one broad customer platform across CRM, marketing, sales, and service, plus a large integration and implementation ecosystem.
- **Why it remains:** HubSpot is a useful benchmark for an integrated revenue stack, while Breeze adds contextual assistants, specialized agents, research, and workflow automation.
- **Avoid or validate when:** hub, seat, credit, implementation, and data-model costs may expand faster than the operating value.

## 02 · Market, account & buyer intelligence

### [Common Room](https://www.commonroom.io/about/?ref=b2b-playbook)

**Type:** AI-native · **Reviewed:** 2026-08-24

- **Use when:** a B2B team needs to combine first-party customer data with product, community, web, social, and other buyer signals.
- **Why it stands out:** it builds a continuously updated buyer-intelligence layer, resolves identities, and lets AI agents prioritize changes and move insights into GTM action.
- **Avoid or validate when:** source provenance, identity resolution, permissioning, regional coverage, or enterprise implementation needs a controlled sample first.

### [Unify](https://www.unifygtm.com/?ref=b2b-playbook)

**Type:** AI-native · **Reviewed:** 2026-08-24

- **Use when:** a team wants intent signals, account research, enrichment, agent qualification, and outbound execution in one signal-driven workflow.
- **Why it stands out:** audiences and plays connect first-party, third-party, and AI-discovered signals to prospecting and multichannel action instead of stopping at an intent score.
- **Avoid or validate when:** credit consumption, signal provenance, mailbox ownership, suppression logic, or automated outreach permissions are not yet governed.

## 03 · Contact data, enrichment & verification

### [Clay](https://www.clay.com/claygent?ref=b2b-playbook)

**Type:** AI-native · **Reviewed:** 2026-08-24

- **Use when:** GTM engineering or operations needs multi-provider enrichment, web research, classification, scoring, routing, and personalized data at scale.
- **Why it stands out:** Claygent combines agents, data providers, tables, workflows, and reasoning traces so teams can express research that a fixed database filter cannot.

### [FullEnrich](https://fullenrich.com/?ref=b2b-playbook)

**Type:** Modern infrastructure · **Reviewed:** 2026-08-24

- **Use when:** a team already has people or accounts and needs a waterfall across multiple providers to find work emails or phone numbers.
- **Why it stands out:** one enrichment layer can query multiple data vendors through a UI, CRM workflow, API, or agent-facing infrastructure.
- **Avoid or validate when:** regional coverage, field-level accuracy, provider provenance, data rights, or unit economics are central; test a representative sample.

### [MoltSets](https://moltsets.com/?ref=b2b-playbook)

**Type:** AI-native · **Reviewed:** 2026-08-24

- **Use when:** an agent, script, Clay table, or n8n workflow needs people and company search, contact enrichment, email validation, mobile numbers, or reverse lookup through API or MCP.
- **Why it stands out:** MoltSets is designed without a traditional dashboard around agent-readable tools, consistent request shapes, risk scores, API access, and an MCP server for Claude, Codex, and other agent workflows.
- **Avoid or validate when:** a beta service, current US-first coverage, fair-use rules, phone-token economics, data provenance, privacy obligations, or production uptime needs deeper review. Benchmark a representative list before replacing other providers.

### [Apollo](https://www.apollo.io/ai?ref=b2b-playbook)

**Type:** Established benchmark · **Reviewed:** 2026-08-24

- **Use when:** a small or mid-market team wants a large B2B database, enrichment, signals, engagement, and embedded AI in one commercial product.
- **Why it remains:** it is a useful bundled baseline against which narrower data, research, and sales-engagement products can be compared.
- **Avoid or validate when:** data accuracy, regional privacy obligations, source transparency, niche coverage, or avoiding an all-in-one dependency matters.

## Selection checklist

- [ ] The operating problem is written without naming a vendor.
- [ ] The product has one primary job and does not appear in multiple categories.
- [ ] An AI-native label is supported by an observable contextual or agentic workflow.
- [ ] Required data sources, permissions, outputs, owners, and failure paths are mapped.
- [ ] A representative sample tests quality before a large import, campaign, or contract.
- [ ] Security, privacy, consent, data rights, and outreach obligations have qualified reviewers.
- [ ] Total cost includes seats, credits, usage, implementation, monitoring, and migration.
- [ ] The team can export its data and unwind the workflow.
- [ ] A pilot has an observable success condition, review date, and stop condition.

Submit additions only when they solve a distinct job, meet the evidence standard, and can be described with a real limitation. A longer list is not automatically a better directory.
