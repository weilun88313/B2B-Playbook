The customer has signed up. Now they need to get something useful done, learn enough to work independently, and know where to turn when they get stuck. Start with that experience; renewals, expansion, and recommendations become more understandable when you know how the customer is doing.

**Last reviewed:** 2026-09-06 · **Reading edit:** 2026-09-12

![Help people get started; Track value and risks; Discuss the next need](/assets/illustrations/08-lifecycle-and-customer-marketing.webp)

## Start here

1. [Help a new customer reach the first useful result](/playbooks/08-lifecycle-and-customer-marketing/customer-onboarding) — Agree on the task, preparation, and responsibilities.
2. [Teach a task people can practice](/playbooks/08-lifecycle-and-customer-marketing/customer-education) — Check what the learner can do afterward.
3. [Prepare a useful renewal conversation](/playbooks/08-lifecycle-and-customer-marketing/renewal-marketing) — Bring the customer’s results and unresolved issues together.
