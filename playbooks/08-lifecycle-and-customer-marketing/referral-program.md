title: "Referral program"

**Published:** 2026-09-12 · **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-12

A customer referral program makes relevant introductions easier and recognizes participation under clear terms. It should preserve the trust between the customer and the person they introduce. A contact list upload is not the same as a willing introduction.

## Choose the referral motion

Decide whether the program seeks an introduction, an eligible trial, or a paid customer. Use a reward event the company can verify and support economically. Keep customer referrals distinct from a professional affiliate program with ongoing publisher promotion.

## Design the exchange

1. **Define a relevant referral.** State customer fit, exclusions, existing-account treatment, and duplicate handling. Make the rules understandable before anyone participates.
2. **Let the recipient choose.** Offer a shareable explanation or an introduction that allows the other person to opt into the conversation. Do not automatically enroll referred people into broad campaigns.
3. **Explain rewards and restrictions.** Specify who receives what, when it is earned, and what happens after refunds or cancellations. Check employer, procurement, disclosure, and applicable legal requirements before rewarding individuals.
4. **Provide a simple handoff.** Give the receiving team context and a timely response obligation. The referrer should not have to manage your sales process.
5. **Validate and fulfill consistently.** Resolve disputes using published rules, document the outcome, and deliver promised rewards. Share only the status information that can appropriately be disclosed to the referrer.
6. **Review customer and recipient experience.** Examine relevance, complaints, qualification, retained value, and operational cost. Incentives that increase low-fit introductions can damage the relationship.

## Worked example

A fictional software customer knows a peer facing the same reporting problem. They share a short product explanation and a referral link. The peer chooses to request an evaluation and sees the relevant reward disclosure.

The program recognizes an eligible purchase after the stated validation period. The referrer receives confirmation of the reward, not confidential details about the peer's contract or usage. If the account was already in an active opportunity, the published rule resolves eligibility.

## Pilot before automating

Run a small number of referrals manually to find confusing terms and handoff gaps. Calculate contribution after rewards and servicing cost. A low-volume program with trusted introductions may be more useful than a heavily promoted incentive that attracts people outside the product's fit.
