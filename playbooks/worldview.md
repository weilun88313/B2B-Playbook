# Worldview

Most growth advice starts with channels. This playbook starts with the
commercial problem: who needs to change, why now, what they use today, and
how a decision gets made.

## The operating model

| Start with | Then decide |
|---|---|
| A specific business problem | Which channel can reach it |
| A named account or segment | Which people matter in the decision |
| A trigger and a time window | What message is relevant now |
| A measurable business outcome | Which activity is worth repeating |

The goal is not to create more activity. The goal is to make the path from
problem recognition to qualified opportunity more predictable.

## Buyer-led by default

The normal decision unit is a buying committee, not an isolated user. A
useful plan accounts for the person who owns the budget, the person who
wants the change, the person who validates the solution, and the person who
can stop the deal.

## Global by design

The frameworks are designed for cross-border B2B work. They separate
universal principles from market-specific assumptions, and they require
local verification for language, regulation, procurement, and business
culture.

Localization is not a translation task. It is the process of making the
problem, proof, workflow, and next step credible to a particular market.

## Evidence over certainty

Every recommendation should be clear about whether it is:

- a documented observation;
- a working hypothesis;
- a repeatable pattern; or
- a conclusion that still needs testing.

An unfinished experiment is more useful than a polished claim with no
evidence.
