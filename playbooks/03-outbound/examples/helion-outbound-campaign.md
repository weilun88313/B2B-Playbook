# Example: Helion outbound campaign (fictional)

**Objective:** Book 20-minute conversations with procurement operations leaders or CPOs three weeks before Industrials Connect.  
**List:** 42 exhibitors and known attending procurement teams; 11 T1, 18 T2, and the rest excluded.  
**Success:** Five qualified conversations, not a target open rate.

## Research summary for one T1 account

- Account: Müller Antriebstechnik (fictional)
- Trigger: official exhibitor directory plus an active Supplier Quality role
- Target seat: Procurement Operations
- Current alternative: questionnaire, spreadsheet, and email follow-up
- Hypothesis: new suppliers signed around the event will increase recertification and audit handoffs
- Next step: a 20-minute comparison focused only on reminders and audit sampling

## First email

**Subject:** Supplier onboarding before Industrials Connect

Mara, I saw Müller in the Industrials Connect exhibitor directory while the team is hiring Supplier Quality. When new suppliers are added around an event, spreadsheet follow-up usually breaks first at reminder ownership and audit sampling; is a 20-minute comparison of that workflow useful before the show?

## Third touch

Add a new reason to respond: a spreadsheet cannot reliably separate factory scope and evidence ownership. If Müller already uses a GRC platform, ask whether supplier reminders live there rather than claiming displacement.

## Close-the-loop condition

Send the final message on day 16 if there is no reply, then stop. Do not place the same contact into an indefinite nurture loop.
