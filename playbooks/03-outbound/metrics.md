# Outbound metrics

## Track these

| Metric | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Qualified conversation rate | Measures conversations that match the ICP and problem, not calendar volume |
| Explicit response rate | Includes referrals, not-now replies, and clear no responses that improve the system |
| T1 → conversation rate | Tests whether one-to-one research earns its cost |
| Trigger performance | Compares observable reasons to act, not subject-line tricks |
| Complaint and opt-out rate | Detects relevance, targeting, and compliance problems |
| Committee expansion | Qualified conversations that reveal another buying seat / qualified conversations |

## Do not count these as outcomes

- Open rate by itself
- Number of contacts uploaded
- Sequence activity without replies
- Meetings with accounts that should have been disqualified
