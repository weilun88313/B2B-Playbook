# Checklist: compliance minimum

This checklist is operational hygiene, not legal advice.

- [ ] The target jurisdiction and channel rules were reviewed.
- [ ] The sender identity and company are clear.
- [ ] The contact source is recorded and explainable.
- [ ] The source did not come from an unverified “email pack” or scraped private area.
- [ ] A valid opt-out path is present when required.
- [ ] Suppressed contacts cannot be re-added by the next import.
- [ ] Complaints and deletion requests have an owner and process.
- [ ] Sensitive or private data is not included in the message or research record.
- [ ] The system records when, why, and through which channel the contact was approached.
- [ ] A qualified reviewer has checked any jurisdiction-specific uncertainty.
