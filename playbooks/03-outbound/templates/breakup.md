# Template: close-the-loop message

Do not manufacture scarcity or guilt. Make the status easy to classify.

> I have not heard back, so I will close this thread rather than keep following up. I reached out because [observable trigger] suggested [specific business job] might be active. If the timing changes around [real future trigger], I am happy to revisit; otherwise no reply is needed.

## Before sending

- Confirm no reply or opt-out was missed.
- Do not add a new pitch to the final message.
- Stop the sequence after sending.
- Record the future trigger only when it is real and explainable.
