# Template: two-sentence outreach

The objective is to open a relevant conversation, not compress a product page into an email.

## Executive version

> [Observable trigger] usually creates [strategic or operating consequence] for [business unit]. We help [peer context] achieve [approved outcome] without [critical trade-off]; worth comparing how you are handling [specific decision] before [dated event]?

## Operator version

> I noticed [verifiable trigger]. Teams handling [business job] with [current alternative] often lose ownership at [specific handoff]; would a short comparison of [narrow workflow] be useful before [date]?

## Guardrails

- Use a subject line that helps the recipient classify the context.
- Remove adjectives that are not supported by evidence.
- Do not invent familiarity, urgency, or a personal detail.
- Ask for the smallest next step that can test the hypothesis.
