# Outbound SOP

## Entry criteria

- The ICP can remove accounts through hard disqualifiers.
- Every T1 account has an observable trigger; T2 accounts share an explicit segment hypothesis.
- The message is written for a buying-committee seat, not a generic persona.

## 1. Research the account

Copy the [account-research template](templates/account-research.md).

- **T1:** one-to-one research.
- **T2:** one shared trigger hypothesis; do not fabricate personalization.
- **T3:** keep out of active outbound.

Research should answer only: why now, who cares, what the likely alternative is, and the smallest useful next step.

For executive sales, target the executive or their direct report. The message should describe the strategic outcome they can defend internally, not a feature inventory. If the company, trigger, and buyer can be replaced without changing the message, the research is not finished.

## 2. Build a short sequence

Copy the [sequence template](templates/sequence.md). Start with five to seven touches across no more than two channels. Every touch must add a reason to respond: a new observation, useful question, evidence, or lower-friction next step.

Cadence follows the buying context. Do not send an empty message merely because “day three” arrived. Always include a respectful close-the-loop message.

## 3. Run both pre-send checks

- [Pre-send](checklists/pre-send.md): role, trigger, first line, claim, and next step.
- [Compliance minimum](checklists/compliance.md): jurisdiction, source, identity, opt-out, suppression, and system traceability.

If either fails, stop. This chapter is not legal advice.

## 4. Classify replies

| Reply | Default action |
|---|---|
| Meeting | Schedule it and resolve buying-committee unknowns |
| Send material | Send only the comparison or constraint page that answers the request |
| Not me | Ask who owns the problem; stop educating the wrong person |
| Not now | Record the trigger and stop the sequence |
| Complaint | Stop immediately and follow the compliance process |

## 5. Change one variable per review

Change the target seat, trigger, first line, offer, or next step—not all of them at once. Otherwise the result cannot teach you what worked.

## Exit criteria

- Every active account has a trigger and target seat.
- The sequence includes a close-the-loop message.
- Success is defined as a qualified conversation, referral, or explicit no—not an open.
