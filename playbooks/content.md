# Content, SEO, GEO

Content is part of the revenue system when it helps a buyer understand a
problem, evaluate alternatives, or make an internal case.

## Score

A page is working if it creates **sales conversations** (demo, sample
list, evaluation thread). Impressions and AI citations are leading
indicators only.

## Start with decision-stage questions

1. Category and role pages
2. Alternative and comparison pages
3. Use-case pages a champion can forward internally
4. Educational pieces that reduce uncertainty

Do not build a publishing calendar in place of answering real buying
questions.

## GEO

Structure pages so a reader or answer engine can find a clear definition,
a number, a comparison, or a decision rule. Specific observations and
first-hand results beat generic prose.

Optimize for being useful in a shortlist, an evaluation thread, or an
internal recommendation.

## Production

One page, one job, one primary question. Ship, record discovery and
pipeline signals in `evidence/`, then iterate. Cadence without evidence
is a blog, not a system.
