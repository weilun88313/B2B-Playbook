# Events

Event-led GTM is a first-class motion in this repo. It is not a brand
activity bolted onto PLG.

The working heuristic (effort, not a claim about ROI math):

- 40% before the show (list, outreach, meetings booked)
- 20% on site (qualification, not badge volume)
- 40% after (conversion with context from the floor)

## Before

1. Pick the show because ICP accounts will be there, not because the
   industry “should attend.”
2. Build the target group from exhibitors, public signals, and — when
   you have it — visitor data. Keep those sources labeled. They are not
   the same evidence.
3. Outreach must mention the show and the reason now. Generic sequences
   attached to an event date still read as generic.

## On site

Qualify against ICP and buying role. A badge is not a lead. Capture the
conversation in language the AE can reuse: problem, timing, who else
cares.

## After

Follow-up in 48 hours with the conversation, not a brochure. If the
show was the trigger, the close date should still be tied to something
in their calendar, not “sometime this quarter.”

## Evidence

Put real show names, what was attempted, and what converted in
`evidence/`. Anonymize accounts. This chapter stays honest only if those
notes exist.
