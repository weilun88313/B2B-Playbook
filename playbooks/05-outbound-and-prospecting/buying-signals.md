**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-30

[Account research](account-research.md) writes one hypothesis for one logo. This page is the **system**: which dated changes you hunt, how hot they are, and what temperature of message they earn. A funding headline pasted into last month’s cold email is not a signal program. It is a news alert with a send button.

This is not a 100-premise catalog, not a vendor skill pack, and not permission to skip the [ICP](../01-strategy-and-buyers/icp.md).

## Use this when

- Every account gets the same first sentence and the team calls it “personalized.”
- A Slack channel of job-change alerts is treated as a queue.
- Marketing and SDRs argue whether a content download is inbound.
- Someone forwarded an 11-play list and asked which one to run this week.

## Do not use this when

- The account would fail the field test without the news. Stay in [ICP](../01-strategy-and-buyers/icp.md).
- You still need the research brief. That is [account research](account-research.md).
- You need the written first touch. That is [cold email](cold-email.md).
- You need the routing integer after they already raised a hand. That is [lead scoring](../09-operations-pipeline-and-measurement/lead-scoring.md).

## Words you will use

| Temperature | Meaning here |
|---|---|
| **Raised-hand** | They asked to evaluate (demo, chat). They already know you. One-to-many is allowed; do not “personalize” a form fill into a cold open. |
| **Engaged** | They know you and did **not** raise a hand (webinar, follower, pricing page without a form). Templated plus one true line. |
| **Premise** | A change *they* made that is not about your marketing (new seat, stack, hire, raise). The sentence is about that change. |
| **Cold** | They do not know you. You picked them. Fully one-to-one, or do not send. |

| Family | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Alumni / former user** | They used you—or a close substitute—somewhere else. Highest trust you did not buy. |
| **New seat** | A leader or owner in role ≤90 days who still has a mandate for an early win. |
| **High-intent site** | Pricing, compare, demo, or integration pages *now*—not a blog bounce. |
| **Stack change** | A tool arrived or left. The work around it is open. |
| **Expansion** | A raise, region, or product line that creates a new job—not “they grew.” |
| **Headcount shock** | Hiring surge or cut that changes how the work gets done. |

A family is a **hunt**, not a play name. Eleven named recipes are a content calendar pretending to be GTM.

## One rule

**A signal changes the week, not the ICP.** If the logo would be a no without the headline, it is still a no. Stack **two independent** facts before you treat it as heat. A vendor reply-rate table is not your SLA.

## Operating method

### Step 1: name the temperature before the sentence

Write: *this person is raised-hand / engaged / premise / cold because _____.* If you cannot finish that, you will send a cold email to a demo request and call it ABM.

### Step 2: pick one family this week

One hunt, one owner, one window. A team that “does signals” by running every play in a skill library will staff a newsroom. Alumni and high-intent site beat a stranger’s funding post. If you cannot name why *this* family moves *this* motion, you are collecting.

### Step 3: write the window—and the stale date

Windows are method, not physics. Defaults you will override with your own log:

| Family | Typical window | Stale when |
|---|---|---|
| Raised-hand / high-intent site | Hours to two days | The page view is last week and nobody wrote why it still matters |
| New seat | After they can take a meeting (often week 2–6), not day 1 | Day 90 with no second fact |
| Expansion / funding | After the announcement circus (often 2–4 weeks) | The raise is a year old |
| Stack change / headcount | 1–2 weeks after the pattern is real | One job post, no second source |
| Alumni | As soon as the new company is in ICP | The person left that category |

Day-1 congratulations to a new VP is how you get ignored. A week-old pricing hit that nobody called is how you donate the meeting to a competitor.

### Step 4: require a second independent fact

Job change plus the same job change scraped twice is one fact. Job change plus a page they hit, a stack you can date, or a seat that already replied is two. Three alerts from one vendor is still one.

### Step 5: refuse the catalog

Do not paste a 30/39/16 trigger list into the CRM. Do not install a Claude skill pack whose first move is another company’s Marketplace. The artifact is a **card**: temperature, family, two facts, window, sentence, stop date. Tools that find the fact live in [TOOLS.md](../../TOOLS.md). This page decides whether the fact is allowed to change the week.

## Teaching fill (invented—not a customer)

A ~400-employee B2B SaaS. New VP Customer Ops (day 22). Careers page shows three security-engineer jobs this month. They are not in our CRM.

| Field | Fill |
|---|---|
| Temperature | Premise (they did not raise a hand; they do not know us). |
| Family | New seat + headcount shock. |
| Fact 1 | VP started 22 days ago (LinkedIn / dated). |
| Fact 2 | Three SecEng jobs this month (careers page). |
| Window | In window. Stale at day 90 or if security freezes vendors. |
| Sentence | About questionnaire-pack turnaround under a new ops owner—not “congrats on the role.” |
| Stop | If the VP is a former customer, rewrite as alumni. If neither fact dates, do not send. |

## Pre-flight checklist

- [ ] The account still passes [ICP](../01-strategy-and-buyers/icp.md) without the headline.
- [ ] Temperature is named before copy.
- [ ] One family is in flight—not eleven.
- [ ] Two independent, dated facts.
- [ ] Window and stale date are written.
- [ ] The sentence is about their change, not our feature.
- [ ] We did not import a trigger library or a marketplace-first skill pack as the program.

## Metrics

| Metric | Diagnostic use |
|---|---|
| Premise confirmation | Replies that confirm the change, not “nice note” |
| In-window send rate | Touches that hit the window you wrote |
| Two-fact rate | Sends that actually stacked |
| Temperature mix | Cold volume crowding out raised-hand and alumni |
| Stale kills | Alerts you correctly refused |

Do not count alerts ingested, plays enabled in a skill repo, or resemblance to someone else’s 35–40% reply table.

## Common mistakes

- Treating a funding announcement as a universal buy.
- Congratulating a new leader on day one.
- Calling a content download inbound.
- Running eleven named plays because a README listed them.
- One scraper, three fields, called “multi-signal.”
- Letting an agent default to a vendor Marketplace before the card exists.
- Skipping [account research](account-research.md) because the alert looked premium.

## Sources and evidence boundary

This is an owner-maintained operating synthesis. It is not a trigger encyclopedia and not a recommended stack.

The four temperatures (raised-hand / engaged / premise / cold), the six hunt families (alumni, new seat, high-intent site, stack change, expansion, headcount), the idea of a window, and “stack two facts” are distilled from a public Claude-skill library for GTM ([ColdIQ GTM Skills](https://github.com/Cold-IQ/ColdIQ-s-GTM-Skills?ref=b2b-playbook)). That repository is a **method prompt**, not a source to copy. Its 137 sales triggers, 34 email templates, 11 named plays, MCP/Marketplace mandate, and reply-rate or ACV multiples are **not** this library’s facts or a recommended operating system. Third-party methodology names used inside that repo stay with their authors.
