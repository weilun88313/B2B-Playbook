# Sales enablement

Marketing that cannot survive a security questionnaire is not B2B
marketing.

## What an AE should be able to send in one thread

- Positioning sentence and contrast page
- Who it is for / not for
- Data sources and confidence labels (especially for event intelligence)
- Security / privacy one-pager and DPA path
- Implementation: what they keep (CRM, sequencer), what you provide
- Proof: a named motion (pre-show list, post-show conversion), not a
  vanity logo wall

## Procurement is a channel

Treat legal and security as a sequence with a owner, not as surprise
latency at the end. Track days-in-legal as a pipeline field.

## Battlecards

Write for the blocker. Incumbent, “we already have a list,” “data
quality,” “we will just scrape the floor plan.” Each card: their claim,
your evidence, the question that moves the champion.

Keep templates in this folder as they are used live. Retire a card when
the objection no longer appears or when its evidence stops being current.
