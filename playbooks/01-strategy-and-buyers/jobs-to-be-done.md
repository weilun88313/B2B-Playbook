Jobs to be done asks what progress someone is trying to make in a particular situation. It helps explain why a buyer changes behavior. A feature request tells you what someone asked for; a job investigation asks what made that request matter.

## Choose a concrete investigation

Use this guide when the same job title appears to want contradictory things, or customers compare your product with an unexpected alternative. Start with recent switching decisions, including customers who stayed with the old approach. Do not ask respondents to invent their ideal future product before understanding the past decision.

## Interview the sequence

1. Establish the circumstances. What was happening at work when the problem became noticeable? What deadline, organizational change or failure made the previous approach less acceptable?
2. Reconstruct the old approach. Ask the person to describe the last real instance of the task. Include spreadsheets, services, colleagues, avoidance and doing nothing.
3. Find the transition. What happened between noticing the problem and actively investigating options? Who introduced a possible solution? What anxiety or dependency nearly stopped the change?
4. Define successful progress in the person's language. Ask what would become easier, safer or possible, how they would recognize success, and who else would judge it. Separate the desired result from a requested interface.
5. Compare accounts for recurring circumstances. Keep conflicting stories. One vivid interview is useful evidence, not a market-wide law. Separate the purchaser's job from the operator's when their goals differ.
6. Turn the hypothesis into a test. Offer a way to complete the task and observe what happens. The test should expose the main uncertainty, such as whether a person will supply data or involve the required colleague.

## Worked example

A fictional finance manager asks for a dashboard. Investigation reveals that she already has reports but cannot explain inconsistent numbers during the board meeting. The desired progress is to defend one reconciled figure before the meeting, not to view more charts.

The team tests a reconciliation walkthrough with source links and exception ownership. If the manager still cannot explain the discrepancies, a visually polished dashboard has not completed the job. If the task is infrequent and an analyst can solve it cheaply, there may be no compelling software purchase.

## A hypothesis you can challenge

```text
When [specific circumstances],
[person or group] needs to [make progress],

Today they use:

```

## Keep the interpretation honest

Do not turn every emotion into a new market category. Preserve the interview's context and distinguish exact quotations from your synthesis. Validate proposed language with the people doing the work, then test behavior. A job statement earns its place when it changes product scope, positioning or research; it is not a slogan to put above unchanged feature copy.
