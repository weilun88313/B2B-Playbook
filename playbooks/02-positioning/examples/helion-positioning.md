# Example: Helion positioning (fictional)

Helion is fictional supplier due-diligence software for manufacturing procurement teams.

## Core sentence

When procurement operations leaders face a customer audit or a surge in new-supplier onboarding, Helion helps them produce an auditable supplier record without replacing the ERP or running another spreadsheet-and-email chase.

## What makes the problem matter

- **Owner:** CPO or Procurement Operations.
- **Daily operator:** Supplier Quality and procurement coordinators.
- **Current alternative:** annual questionnaires, spreadsheets, email reminders, and outside counsel.
- **Cost of staying the same:** missed supplier deadlines, unclear ownership, and weak audit traceability.
- **Trigger:** a dated customer audit, supplier onboarding wave, or GRC renewal.
- **Constraint:** IT will check data residency, SSO, and the ERP boundary.

## Board-level version

> This lets procurement add suppliers faster without losing the audit trail customers require.

## 20-second spoken version

“When a customer audit lands six weeks after a supplier-sourcing event, spreadsheet follow-up usually breaks first. Helion isolates onboarding and recertification so procurement can show who supplied what evidence without replacing the ERP. Is that the part of the audit process your team still owns manually?”

## Comparison-page direction

Compare Helion with “questionnaire + spreadsheet + email,” not an invented category enemy. The manual approach is stronger for a low-volume, one-time review; Helion becomes relevant when multiple entities, recurring recertification, and audit handoffs make ownership visible.
