# Positioning SOP

## Entry criteria

You have a draft ICP and can name the current alternative, including “do nothing.” Calling the alternative “the traditional way” is not specific enough.

## 1. Write the trigger, not the feature

A trigger is observable: a deadline, renewal, event, hiring pattern, regulation, incident, or failed audit. A feature is not a trigger.

## 2. Complete one positioning sentence

Copy the [positioning-sentence template](templates/positioning-sentence.md):

> When [specific buyer] faces [trigger], [category] helps them [business result] without [critical cost or trade-off].

Then answer:

1. Why is the problem expensive, urgent, or strategically important?
2. Who owns the outcome and who carries the daily work?
3. What is the current alternative, including inaction?
4. What evidence makes a change credible?

For executive buyers, apply the board-level test: what one sentence would they use to explain the change to a CEO or COO? That sentence should describe a strategic advantage, not another AI task list.

## 3. Run the homepage 10-second test

Use [homepage-10s.md](checklists/homepage-10s.md). A reader who never opens the blog should still see what the product is, who it is for, what it replaces, the outcome, the next step, and one operational constraint such as security, deployment, data, integration, or implementation effort.

## 4. Build comparison pages only for real buying decisions

Copy the [comparison-page outline](templates/contrast-page-outline.md). Create a page only when sales repeatedly encounters that alternative. Include where the alternative is stronger; refusing to name its strengths makes the page less credible and less useful to a champion.

## 5. Synchronize the spoken version

Turn the same positioning into a 20-second verbal story: trigger → crack in the alternative → result → one confirmation question. The homepage, first outbound paragraph, and demo opening should share this structure.

## Exit criteria

- A non-founder can repeat the positioning sentence.
- Every unchecked homepage item is either fixed or intentionally excluded.
- At least one comparison-page outline addresses an alternative that appears in real deals.
