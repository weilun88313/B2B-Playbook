# 06 · Enterprise Sales

Enterprise sales is not the five CRM buckets—intro, demo, proposal, contract, won/lost. Those are forecasting states. The buyer experiences a longer sequence: access, an unrecorded first conversation, demo co-design, a possible pre-demo, the group demo, immediate debrief, pricing, pilot design, procurement, paper, and signature.

This module covers the path from the first meeting to signature while preserving information and helping the internal champion buy rather than feel sold to.

## Use this when

- The deal is valuable and complex enough to require executive approval.
- An executive or direct report can enter the process.
- Security, implementation, pilot, procurement, or legal review shapes the decision.

## Do not use this when

- Discovery is a scripted BANT interview.
- The seller demos before understanding the decision.
- The deal cannot support a multi-meeting buying process.
