# Enterprise sales metrics

Use ranges as diagnostic hypotheses, not quotas or promises.

## Funnel health after qualification

| Signal | Question when it is weak |
|---|---|
| Right seat → qualified opportunity | Is the entry seat wrong, or is the story too tactical? |
| Discovery → co-designed demo | Is the seller giving away the demo before earning context? |
| Demo → pilot or commercial process | Did the demo prove a narrow decision or expose irrelevant product surface? |
| Pilot → next buyer action | Was the pilot designed before timing and success were agreed? |
| Qualified opportunity → signature | Is qualification too loose, pricing misaligned, or the decision process unmanaged? |

Public interview ranges sometimes place qualified-opportunity-to-signature near 25–35% and pilot progression much higher. Treat those figures as starting questions; validate them against your segment, deal definition, and data quality.

## Process health

- Number of buyer working sessions between the first call and group demo
- Share of demos with a documented, account-specific scope
- Pilots opened only after success and signature timing are written
- Production work started before signature (target: zero)
- Opportunities with named procurement, legal, security, and signatory owners

## Do not count these as progress

- Moving a CRM stage because a demo occurred
- Demo volume
- Pilot logins without agreed task completion
- A very high win rate without checking price and qualification quality
