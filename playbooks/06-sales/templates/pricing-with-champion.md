# Template: pricing conversation with the champion

## Context

- Agreed outcome:
- Scope:
- Implementation work:
- Current alternative and cost:
- Commercial timing:

## Questions

1. How would you explain the purchase internally?
2. Which part of the value is easiest to defend?
3. What price or term would be difficult to carry, and why?
4. Is the constraint budget, scope, timing, implementation, or confidence?
5. What would need to be true for the preferred scope to work?

## Options to consider

- Adjust scope without weakening the success condition.
- Stage implementation across year one and year two.
- Change term or payment timing when commercially responsible.
- Keep the price and add proof or risk reduction.

Do not default to discounting before the constraint is named.
