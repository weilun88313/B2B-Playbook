## Opening

> We may not need the full time. Let us first compare what is changing in your business and how you handle it today; if there is no meaningful fit, we can stop there.

## Questions

1. What must be different next year from this year?
2. Why does that need to change now?
3. How is the outcome measured today?
4. What process or tool handles the work now?
5. Who owns the result, and who carries the daily work?
6. Who would need to support or approve a change?
7. What could make the timing too early?

## Seller story

Explain only the parts that respond to the buyer's frame. After each part, ask what fits, what does not, and what remains missing.
