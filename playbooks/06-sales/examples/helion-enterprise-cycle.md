# Example: Helion enterprise cycle (fictional)

Account: Müller Antriebstechnik. Use case: supplier onboarding and recertification. Illustrative annual scope: €120,000.

## Executive and direct-report entry

The founder approaches the CPO with the auditability outcome. The enterprise seller approaches Mara, the Procurement Operations lead. The CPO replies, “Work with Mara,” while Mara is already engaged, so both join the first conversation.

## Informal first conversation

No demo. Mara explains that the team wants to reduce dependence on outside-counsel questionnaires and bring onboarding ownership back inside procurement. “Why now?” reveals a customer audit six weeks after an industry event. The seller discusses only reminders and audit sampling.

## Demo co-design

The group demo needs the CPO, Mara, Supplier Quality, and IT Security. Mara chooses reminder ownership and factory-level scope as the two product areas. IT receives a short pre-demo on data residency so security does not take over the group session.

## Group demo and debrief

The seller re-opens in the customer's language, demonstrates only the two chosen workflows, and parks ERP integration for a separate session. Immediately afterward, Mara notes that the Quality Director remained unconvinced, so a focused 15-minute conversation is scheduled.

## Pricing

The group receives a bounded €150k–€200k range with scope assumptions. In the one-to-one conversation, Mara says the CFO will resist the upper end. Instead of discounting immediately, the team reduces year-one deployment scope and commits to a year-two path.

## Pilot

The audit is six weeks away. A 48-hour pilot includes Mara and three procurement operators. The tasks are to chase one overdue supplier, export one audit sample, and separate scope for a second factory. The CPO remains sponsor rather than user.

## Procurement and signature

An editable agreement is sent. Procurement requests a high insurance level; counsel resolves the material issue live. The CFO is confirmed as signatory before routing. No production data is loaded before signature.
