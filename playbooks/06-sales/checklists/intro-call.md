# Checklist: intro call

- [ ] The attendee is an executive, direct report, or an explicitly useful discovery seat.
- [ ] The seller can state the observable trigger and account hypothesis.
- [ ] The meeting can end early if there is no meaningful change to discuss.
- [ ] The buyer speaks before the product story begins.
- [ ] Questions explore what must change, why now, and how the outcome is measured.
- [ ] The current alternative is named.
- [ ] Missing buying-committee seats are recorded.
- [ ] No demo is promised before the room and scope are understood.
- [ ] Recording and consent follow company policy and applicable rules.
- [ ] The next step tests maturity rather than assumes a deal.
