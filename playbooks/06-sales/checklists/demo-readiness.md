# Checklist: demo readiness

- [ ] The champion helped choose the demo scope.
- [ ] Every invited seat has a reason to attend.
- [ ] The opening uses the customer's language and outcome.
- [ ] The two or three product areas that matter are explicit.
- [ ] Topics to park for later are listed.
- [ ] The seller knows which objection or unknown the demo must resolve.
- [ ] New attendees will be asked what they need from the session.
- [ ] The champion has an active role in the meeting.
- [ ] A post-demo debrief with the champion is planned.
- [ ] Security, data, integration, and implementation questions have owners.
