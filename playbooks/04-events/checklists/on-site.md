# Checklist: on site

- [ ] Start with the business job and timing, not the product tour.
- [ ] Ask how the work is handled today.
- [ ] Capture the dated trigger or write that none exists.
- [ ] Record the current alternative.
- [ ] Identify who else cares and who can block change.
- [ ] Distinguish a badge scan from a substantive conversation.
- [ ] Agree on the smallest next step while context is fresh.
- [ ] Assign an owner and follow-up deadline.
- [ ] Do not photograph, export, or share private attendee information without authorization.
