# Event-led growth SOP

## Entry criteria

The team can use the ICP to determine whether relevant accounts and buying-committee seats are likely to appear. If not, complete [01 · ICP](../01-icp/) before purchasing a booth.

## 1. Decide whether to go and at what level

Copy the [event scorecard](templates/event-scorecard.md). A low score should produce a smaller commitment: skip, run remote pre-event outreach, attend with one person, or avoid exhibiting.

## 2. Before the event: list, meetings, and story

Run the [pre-event checklist](checklists/pre-show.md).

- Label every target source: exhibitor directory, public speaker list, professional-network signal, or organizer/partner attendee data. These sources carry different evidence.
- Filter the list through the ICP before enrichment or outreach.
- Tie outreach to the real event context and trigger. Adding an event date to a generic sequence does not make it event-led.
- Measure meetings booked before opening day, not “we will see who stops by.”

## 3. On site: qualify, do not collect badges

Run the [on-site checklist](checklists/on-site.md). Capture what a seller can continue later: business job, timing, current alternative, next decision, and missing committee seats.

Choose two questions:

1. How do you handle this today?
2. What deadline or change brought this issue into focus now?
3. Who could stop a change when you return?

A badge is an identity record, not a qualified lead.

## 4. Follow up within 48 hours

Run the [post-event checklist](checklists/post-show.md) and use the [follow-up template](templates/post-show-followup.md).

Reference the actual conversation. Attach the next step to the buyer's calendar—a review, budget window, renewal, or another event—not an arbitrary seller quarter. Downgrade badges with no conversation instead of pretending they are warm leads.

## Exit criteria

- The go / attend / exhibit / skip decision has a score and owner.
- A target list and pre-booked meeting goal exist before opening day.
- Every qualified conversation receives contextual follow-up within 48 hours.
- The review separates sourced targets, badge scans, qualified conversations, and opportunities.
