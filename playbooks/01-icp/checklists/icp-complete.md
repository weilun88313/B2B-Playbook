# Checklist: is the ICP complete?

- [ ] The ICP names a business job, not a product category.
- [ ] The operating scale can be observed or verified.
- [ ] At least one current-quarter trigger can be observed.
- [ ] At least five hard disqualifiers exist.
- [ ] The current alternative process or tool is named.
- [ ] Economic buyer, champion, technical buyer, and blocker are named or marked unknown.
- [ ] Every unknown seat has a discovery action.
- [ ] Five recent accounts have pressure-tested the rules.
- [ ] Tiering leads to different default actions.
- [ ] Facts, assumptions, and unknowns are visibly separated.
