# Example: Helion ICP (fictional)

Helion sells supplier due-diligence software to manufacturing procurement teams. Every company, person, and number below is fictional and demonstrates completion density only.

## One sentence

Prioritize procurement operations leaders or CPOs at discrete manufacturers in Europe or the United States when a customer audit or supplier-onboarding deadline exposes the limits of annual questionnaires, spreadsheets, and email follow-up.

## Four-part definition

| Area | Definition | Observable filter |
|---|---|---|
| Industry and job | Automotive components, industrial equipment, and electronics; new-supplier onboarding and annual recertification | Industry codes, event directories, procurement hiring |
| Operating scale | 500–5,000 employees, at least three operating entities, and a dedicated procurement function | Employee range, facility count, procurement roles |
| Trigger | Customer audit, new supplier sourcing around an industry event, or a current GRC contract nearing renewal | Job posts, exhibitor lists, dated audit or renewal evidence |
| Disqualifiers | No manufacturing footprint, no dedicated purchasing function, consulting-only request, unsupported deployment requirement | Manual review |

## Typical committee

| Seat | Common role | Primary concern |
|---|---|---|
| Economic buyer | CPO or CFO | Audit exposure and total operating cost |
| Champion | Procurement Operations or Supplier Quality | Cycle time and supplier follow-up |
| Technical buyer | IT Security or Enterprise Applications | SSO, residency, ERP boundaries |
| Blocker | Current GRC owner or questionnaire administrator | Workflow ownership and reporting definitions |

## Two fictional accounts

**Müller Antriebstechnik:** 1,200 employees, three plants, hiring Supplier Quality, and listed for an upcoming industrial event. Fit 2 + budget 2 + alternative 2 + trigger 2 + committee 1 = 9 → **T1**.

**Shoply:** an 80-person direct-to-consumer brand where operations handles purchasing part-time. It matches two hard disqualifiers → **T3 without scoring**.
