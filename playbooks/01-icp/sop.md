# ICP SOP

## Entry criteria

For the last ten serious conversations, you can name the contact's role, the company's operating context, and how they solve the problem today. If you cannot, run interviews before creating a profile.

## 1. Write who you will not sell to

Begin with five hard disqualifiers: no purchasing function, an unsupported contracting model, no budget owner, a use case outside the product's scope, or a decision process in a market you cannot serve.

Exclusions are more useful than attractive adjectives because they immediately remove accounts from the list.

## 2. Complete the four-part ICP

Copy the [ICP canvas](templates/icp-canvas.md). Make each section specific enough to filter a list:

1. industry and business job;
2. operating scale and budget ownership;
3. an observable current-quarter trigger;
4. hard disqualifiers.

Labels such as “SMB,” “mid-market,” or “enterprise” are not filters by themselves.

## 3. Map the buying committee

Copy the [buying-committee template](templates/buying-committee.md). For each active account, record the economic buyer, champion, technical buyer, and blocker. If a seat is unknown, record how it will be discovered.

A champion without an economic buyer is a conversation, not yet a qualified deal.

## 4. Tier accounts and prescribe an action

Copy the [account scorecard](templates/account-scorecard.md).

| Tier | Meaning | Default action |
|---|---|---|
| T1 | High fit and an observable trigger | One-to-one research and a personalized next step |
| T2 | Fit exists but the trigger or evidence is weak | Test one shared hypothesis across a small segment |
| T3 | Low fit, hard exclusion, or insufficient evidence | Do not enter active outbound |

Tiers allocate time; they are not moral judgments about a company.

## 5. Pressure-test with five real accounts

Score five recent accounts. If the companies you want to pursue and the companies that actually progress do not match, revise the ICP rather than changing weights to protect a preference.

## Exit criteria

- The one-page ICP contains observable filters.
- At least five hard disqualifiers exist.
- Every buying-committee seat has a name or a discovery action.
- Two teammates can independently tier the same list with similar results.
