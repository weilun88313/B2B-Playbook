A lead magnet offers a useful resource in exchange for information or a requested follow-up. The exchange should be proportionate and understandable. A form is justified by the experience it enables, not by the desire to count another lead.

## Decide whether to gate

An ungated checklist may reach more people and still support buying. Consider a form when delivery, customization, participation, or a clearly requested relationship requires information. If the resource is generic and easy to provide openly, test whether gating adds enough value to justify the friction.

## Design the exchange

1. **Choose a concrete task.** Promise something the reader can use: a worksheet, calculation, diagnostic, or substantive guide. Avoid a title whose value disappears after submission.
2. **Show enough before the form.** Explain the contents, intended audience, limitations, and a sample. Let readers judge relevance without surrendering information first.
3. **Minimize required fields.** Ask only for information needed for the stated purpose. Make optional requests explicit and explain any follow-up. Applicable privacy and marketing rules still need review.
4. **Deliver immediately and reliably.** Test the download, email, or access path on common devices. Include accessible labels and useful error messages. Do not require a surprise sales call to receive the promised resource.
5. **Separate interest from qualification.** A download establishes interest in the resource, not budget, authority, or permission for unrelated marketing. Route explicit consultation requests separately.
6. **Evaluate the entire exchange.** Review valid access, actual use, feedback, complaints, and later relevant progression. Compare gated and ungated approaches with their different reach and measurement limitations.

## Worked example

A fictional vendor offers a migration-risk worksheet. The preview shows the questions and a completed fictional row. The file is available after a short delivery form; newsletter enrollment is a separate, clearly described option.

The team finds that many users want the worksheet but few want a consultation. It improves the worksheet and invitation rather than labeling all downloaders sales-ready. A later ungated version can test whether the wider access better serves the business and reader.

## Before publishing

Submit the form as a new user, trigger validation errors, and confirm that the promised asset arrives. Remove any mismatch between the page promise and the actual file. The resource should be worth using even if the reader never becomes a customer.
