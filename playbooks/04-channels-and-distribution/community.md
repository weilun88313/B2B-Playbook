**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-30

For high-ACV B2B, **one credible intro inside a room that already debates the job** usually beats a month of generic reach. [Ecosystem](../06-account-field-and-partner/ecosystem.md) is the company flywheel. This page is the **room**: which existing vertical community we enter, what we contribute before we extract, and what “peer endorsement” is allowed to mean.

This is not “build our own Slack so we can call it community-led.” That asset, if you ever own it, is still planned. It is also not a paid-community membership pitch.

## Use this when

- ACV is high enough that one trusted peer changes the quarter more than a thousand impressions.
- Buyers already sit in a named operator room, association chapter, or practitioner forum.
- The default next spend is an ad in that room’s media kit.
- Leadership wants “community” and means a logo on a Slack sidebar.

## Do not use this when

- The ICP and alternative are empty. Stay in [ICP](../01-strategy-and-buyers/icp.md).
- You do not yet have ~10 matching customers. Handmade rings are [first ten customers](../01-strategy-and-buyers/first-ten-customers.md).
- The primary motion is unnamed. [Channel strategy](channel-strategy.md) first.
- You intend to scrape the member directory. That is an incident, not a motion.

## Words you will use

| Word | Meaning here |
|---|---|
| **Room** | An existing community the buyer already chose—not your branded Discord. |
| **Composition** | Share of *their* members who are *our* ICP. Follower count is not this. |
| **Contribution** | Something the room can use without buying you: AMA, shared template, data cut, small table. |
| **Endorsement** | A named peer (or the room’s host) putting their reputation on an intro. A pin is not this. |
| **Extraction** | Asking for pipeline before the room has a reason to keep you. |

## One rule

**Contribute before you extract.** If the first artifact is an ad, a member-list export, or a “quick hello” blast, you are renting a hallway. High ACV buys patience: one intro from a peer who did the work is the unit. A sponsored slot is the last resort, not the opener.

## Operating method

### Step 1: name the room from where they already learn

Write: *this seat already debates [job] in [room] because [evidence].* If the evidence is “everyone is on LinkedIn,” you do not have a room. You have a network. Coverage × composition still apply—see [ecosystem](../06-account-field-and-partner/ecosystem.md). High composition and modest coverage is the usual high-ACV wedge.

### Step 2: pick one contribution, not a campaign calendar

Default order—ads are not on this list:

| Contribution | Use when | Stop if |
|---|---|---|
| **AMA / office hours** | You can answer operating questions without a pitch deck | The host has to remind you not to demo |
| **Template co-create** | The room already uses a messy version of a working file | You ship a branded PDF they will not edit |
| **Data cut / short report** | You have a dated, checkable slice the room cannot get elsewhere | The cut is a product brochure with a chart |
| **Small roundtable** | Eight to twelve people, one seat, one question | It becomes a mixer so more logos can “join” |

Do the smallest thing the host would thank you for in public. Then stop. A second contribution is earned, not scheduled.

### Step 3: write what endorsement is allowed to be

Allowed: a host or member intro to a named account, a “I used this when _____” line a champion can forward, a seat at a table you did not buy. Not allowed: quoting the community as a customer, implying the room vets vendors, or turning a private thread into a case study.

### Step 4: attach the intro to a motion you already run

The intro is not a channel. It is a **warm ring** into [account research](../05-outbound-and-prospecting/account-research.md), a [dinner](../06-account-field-and-partner/executive-dinners.md), or a page from [content strategy](../03-brand-story-and-content/content-strategy.md). If sales cannot finish “I use this room when _____,” you collected members.

### Step 5: refuse the media kit until the room has used you

If they offer a newsletter blast, a Slack sponsor, or a logo wall, treat it as paid social with extra guilt. Buy it only after a contribution has produced a dated intro or a forwarded artifact—and only with a stop rule.

## Teaching fill (invented—not a customer)

Ops leaders in a 400-person practitioner room. They already share questionnaire-pack templates. We do not advertise. We co-edit one working file they already circulate; the host introduces us to two T1 accounts who asked for the next version.

| Field | Fill |
|---|---|
| Room | Named ops chapter; not “B2B Twitter.” |
| Composition | High: heads of customer ops, 200–800 employees. Coverage modest. |
| Contribution | Template co-create (one file). Not an AMA until they ask. |
| Endorsement we will accept | Host intro, or a member’s “we used the pack when _____.” |
| Extract we will not do | Member CSV, sponsored thread, “community case study.” |
| Next motion | Two accounts → [account research](../05-outbound-and-prospecting/account-research.md). |

## Pre-flight checklist

- [ ] The room exists without us.
- [ ] Composition is written; vanity reach is not the reason.
- [ ] One contribution is named; ads are not the first move.
- [ ] Endorsement rules are written before anyone posts.
- [ ] Sales can finish “I use this room when _____.”
- [ ] We will not scrape, blast, or quote the room as a customer.
- [ ] ACV and cycle justify the patience this motion needs.

## Metrics

| Metric | Diagnostic use |
|---|---|
| Peer intros | Named-account intros a seller would recognize without the host in the room |
| Champion forwards | Times the contribution is pasted into an evaluation thread |
| Contribution used | Rooms that reused the artifact without being asked |
| Media-kit restraint | Sponsored slots we correctly refused |
| Composition drift | Members in the last table who were not the seat |

Do not count Slack joins, emoji, AMA attendance, or “community impressions.”

## Common mistakes

- Building a branded community because entering an existing one felt slow.
- Buying the sponsor package as the handshake.
- Treating a pin or a logo as endorsement.
- Inviting every persona so the room “scales.”
- Scraping members into outbound.
- Skipping [channel strategy](channel-strategy.md) and calling community the primary motion.

This is an owner-maintained operating synthesis. It is not a membership to buy and not a scrape license.

Contribute-before-extract, composition over reach, and “one peer intro beats generic traffic at high ACV” are operating judgments in this library. Operator rooms often report that role-consistent, smaller formats outperform large mixers; treat that as a **method prompt**, not a community’s pipeline math.
