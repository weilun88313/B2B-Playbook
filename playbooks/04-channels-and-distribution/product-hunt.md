# Product Hunt

**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-30

Product Hunt is a **one-day concentrator**: launch energy, comments, and a screenshot other people can forward. It is not a growth motion. [Channel strategy](channel-strategy.md) still names the primary. [First ten customers](../01-strategy-and-buyers/first-ten-customers.md) already refuses to let Product Hunt be week-one.

A good day can mint social proof. A standing “we grow on PH” plan is how teams confuse upvotes with a market.

## Use this when

- You have a working product a hunter can try the same day.
- You can staff comments, support, and a founder who will stay on the thread.
- You want a dated public spike you can point at—not a pipeline forecast.
- The alternative is a quiet launch with no artifact a champion can send.

## Do not use this when

- The product cannot be used without a sales call. You will collect tourists.
- You need the first ten logos. Stay in [first ten customers](../01-strategy-and-buyers/first-ten-customers.md).
- You are buying upvotes, fake comments, or a “we will get you #1” service.
- Leadership wants Product Hunt as the Q3 acquisition channel.

## Words you will use

| Word | Meaning here |
|---|---|
| **Launch day** | One calendar date with a hunter, a thread, and a stop time. |
| **Amplifier** | Attention and proof. Not a repeating source of qualified pipeline. |
| **Hunter** | The person who posts. Not a vendor who sells a ranking. |
| **Proof** | A dated page, comment, or press mention a champion can forward. |
| **Tourist** | A signup that will never match [ICP](../01-strategy-and-buyers/icp.md). |

## One rule

**Budget one day, then close the motion.** Harvest the questions into [content strategy](../03-brand-story-and-content/content-strategy.md). Do not leave a standing campaign, a weekly hunter, or a KPI called “PH traffic.” If the day does not produce a usable proof artifact, you had a party.

## Operating method

### Step 1: write what the day is allowed to produce

Pick one: a proof URL, a set of buyer questions, a list of people who already used the product, or a “we launched” line for later [creator](creator-partnership.md) pieces. If the sentence is “leads,” you are in the wrong playbook—use [lead scoring](../09-operations-pipeline-and-measurement/lead-scoring.md) only after you know they are not tourists.

### Step 2: make the product survive strangers

Same-day value, or a honest “request access” with no fake self-serve. Support and comments have an owner from launch to +24 hours. A founder welcome to people who *used* it is allowed. A sequence to everyone who upvoted is not.

### Step 3: staff the thread like a room, not like ads

Real users comment first. You answer operating questions. You do not argue with the ranking. You do not seed fake customers. [Peer community](community.md) rules still apply: no scrape of the hunter list into outbound.

### Step 4: next morning, sort

| Keep | Kill |
|---|---|
| Questions that belong on a decision page | “PH” as a CRM source you will optimize |
| Named users who match ICP | Tourist signups in the hunter queue |
| One proof sentence with a date | A plan to launch again next month to “keep growing” |

Loom-shaped days in the first-ten interviews were **handmade follow-up**, not a channel. Copy the follow-up discipline, not the upvote count.

## Teaching fill (invented—not a customer)

A self-serve tool. Launch Tuesday. Hunter is a customer who already used it. Goal = a dated proof page and five ICP questions, not MQLs. Morning after: 12 tourists suppressed; three ICP users get a founder note; PH is not a source we report next quarter.

## Pre-flight checklist

- [ ] Primary motion is named and is not Product Hunt.
- [ ] A stranger can get value the same day, or we said they cannot.
- [ ] Hunter is real; we are not buying the rank.
- [ ] Comments and support have an owner.
- [ ] Tourist suppression is written.
- [ ] We know the one proof artifact we will keep.
- [ ] First-ten work is not waiting on this day.

## Metrics

| Metric | Diagnostic use |
|---|---|
| ICP users from the day | People who match the field test, not upvotes |
| Questions harvested | New rows on the decision-page map |
| Proof used | Times sales pasted the dated launch into a thread |
| Tourist rate | Signups we correctly suppressed |
| Motion closed | Whether PH is still a standing KPI (it should not be) |

Do not count upvotes, #1, or referral traffic as acquisition.

## Common mistakes

- Making Product Hunt the week-one plan.
- Buying the rank.
- Dumping upvoters into SDR sequences.
- Leaving PH on the dashboard as a channel.
- Launching a sales-assist product as if it were self-serve.
- Treating a spike as [product-market fit](../01-strategy-and-buyers/product-market-fit.md).

## What to read next

Handmade first logos are [first ten customers](../01-strategy-and-buyers/first-ten-customers.md). Whether anyone stays is [product-market fit](../01-strategy-and-buyers/product-market-fit.md). The motion you return to is [channel strategy](channel-strategy.md). Questions from the thread become [content strategy](../03-brand-story-and-content/content-strategy.md). If you needed a peer room instead of a launch day, that is [peer community](community.md).

## Sources and evidence boundary

This is an owner-maintained operating synthesis. Product Hunt is a third-party surface; rankings, fees, and rules change.

Named Product Hunt episodes in this library (for example Loom in the first-ten interviews) stay with those dated sources. They illustrate a spike plus handmade follow-up, not a repeatable acquisition engine.
