# Outbound

Outbound is a focused conversation with a relevant person at a relevant
account. Email and professional networks are tools, not the strategy.

## Before a sequence

- Account matches ICP and a trigger exists (event, hiring, tech change,
  public initiative).
- You know which seat you are writing to. A champion email is not an
  economic-buyer email.
- The first line is about their world. Product is the second or third
  sentence, if it appears at all.

## Sequence design

Keep the first sequence short enough to learn from. Five to seven touches
across two appropriate channels is a reasonable starting point, but timing
should follow the buying context rather than an arbitrary cadence.

Every touch should add a reason to reply: a useful observation, a relevant
question, proof, or a low-friction next step.

Breakup mail is required. Silence is a result.

## Compliance (non-negotiable)

Cross-border outreach requires local verification:

- B2B outreach rules differ by country and channel. Check the applicable
  requirements before the send.
- Honor opt-out immediately.
- Keep a record of the source, purpose, and lawful basis for contact data
  where required.
- Do not scrape or send from systems you cannot explain in a security
  review.

Do not turn this file into legal advice. Record verified, market-specific
notes in `evidence/` and ask qualified counsel when the risk is material.

## Message structure

1. Context: why this account or person is relevant.
2. Observation: what changed or what may be inefficient.
3. Relevance: how the problem maps to the outcome you support.
4. Proof: one specific, credible signal.
5. Ask: one small next step.

The objective is not to maximize sends. It is to find out whether the
problem, timing, and buyer are real.
