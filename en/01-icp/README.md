# 01 · ICP & Buying Committee

Turn a market into **accounts and people**, not “everyone interested in this category.”

## Use this when

- Sales is spending time on companies that are unlikely to buy.
- Content and outbound target a different audience in every channel.
- A deal has a friendly user but no visible economic buyer.

## Do not use this when

- You have fewer than eight serious customer conversations. Interview first; do not fill a scorecard from imagination.
- You want an ICP document to prove that everyone can be a customer.
