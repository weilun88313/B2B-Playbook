# ICP metrics

## Track these

| Metric | Calculation | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| T1 share | T1 accounts in active outbound / all active outbound accounts | A low share means personalized effort is being wasted |
| Disqualifier hit rate | Accounts removed by rules / new accounts reviewed | A rule that removes nothing is probably not operating |
| Committee completeness | T1 opportunities with a named economic buyer or blocker / all T1 opportunities | Champion-only pipelines are inflated |
| Tier agreement | Accounts receiving the same tier from two independent reviewers / accounts reviewed | A non-repeatable rule cannot support teamwork |

## Do not count these as outcomes

- “The ICP workshop is complete.”
- Number of persona pages.
- An unfiltered TAM estimate.

The default thresholds are operating heuristics. Validate them against your own wins, losses, and disqualifications.
