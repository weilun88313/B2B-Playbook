Illustration direction
======================

These original AI-generated English article illustrations were created for
B2B Playbook with the built-in image generation tool. Their visual direction
adapts the Ian Xiaohei Illustrations skill by Ian (helloianneo):
https://github.com/helloianneo/ian-xiaohei-illustrations?ref=b2b-playbook
Reference revision: 91b560849e8f883922cc2fa8a358a668caa94105

The recurring Xiaohei character and hand-drawn visual language are credited
to Ian. The bundled example compositions were not copied. Images explain
concepts; they are not documentary depictions of named companies or people.
The website keeps its violet Luma identity; article artwork retains a white
background with black lines and sparse blue/orange English annotations.

Original PNGs and generation records are retained separately. WebP editions
are compressed reading assets, exported at exactly 1600 x 900 pixels.
