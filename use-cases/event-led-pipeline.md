# Use case: event-led pipeline

**Evidence:** Fictional worked example  
**Company:** Helion  
**Event:** Industrials Connect  
**Goal:** Decide whether to attend and turn the event into qualified next actions

## 1. Go / attend / skip decision

| Criterion | Score 0–2 | Evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| ICP concentration | 2 | 42 of 120 exhibitors match the working ICP |
| Buying-committee access | 2 | Procurement and supplier-quality leaders are listed |
| Trigger density | 2 | Supplier sourcing and audit preparation are explicit themes |
| Pre-booking potential | 2 | Eleven T1 accounts have visible roles or warm paths |
| Cost and capacity | 1 | Travel is justified; a large booth is not |
| **Total** | **9/10** | **Attend with a meeting-led plan** |

Pre-booked qualified-meeting target: eight. The decision would become **skip** if account concentration or pre-booking access proved false.

## 2. Before the event

The team segments the directory:

- 120 exhibitors reviewed;
- 42 ICP matches;
- 11 T1 accounts receive one-to-one research;
- T2 accounts receive one shared trigger hypothesis;
- six qualified meetings are booked before opening day.

Every meeting brief records the account, person, buying seat, trigger, current alternative, evidence, unknowns, and intended next step.

## 3. On site

One fictional conversation:

- **Seat:** Procurement Operations at Müller Antriebstechnik
- **Current alternative:** spreadsheet plus email reminders
- **Trigger:** customer audit six weeks after the event
- **Missing seat:** IT Security
- **Next step:** a 30-minute remote review of reminder ownership and audit sampling

The team records next steps during the conversation. Badge scans without context do not enter the qualified pipeline.

## 4. Follow-up

**Subject:** Industrials Connect — customer audit in six weeks

> Mara, at Industrials Connect you said annual recertification still runs through a spreadsheet and the customer audit is six weeks away. The useful next step is a 30-minute review of reminder ownership and the fields the audit team will sample. If data residency is already a concern, please include IT Security.

## 5. Review

The attendance decision was correct; six meetings were booked; nine qualified conversations occurred; security coverage was the recurring gap; the next event plan must identify the technical seat before opening day.
