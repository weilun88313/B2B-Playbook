# Use case: enterprise pilot

**Evidence:** Fictional worked example  
**Company:** Helion  
**Account:** Müller Antriebstechnik  
**Goal:** Move from executive access to a decision-specific pilot and signed agreement

## Executive and champion entry

Helion's founder approaches the CPO with an auditability outcome. The enterprise seller approaches Mara, the Procurement Operations lead. The CPO replies, “Work with Mara,” while Mara is already engaged, so both join the first conversation.

## First conversation

No demo is shown. Mara explains that the team wants to reduce dependence on outside-counsel questionnaires and bring onboarding ownership back inside procurement. “Why now?” reveals a customer audit six weeks after an industry event.

The seller discusses only reminder ownership and audit sampling. Missing seats—Supplier Quality and IT Security—become explicit discovery work.

## Demo co-design

Mara chooses reminder ownership and factory-level scope as the two product areas that matter. The group demo includes the CPO, Mara, Supplier Quality, and IT Security. IT receives a short pre-demo on data residency so security does not take over the group session.

The formal demo proves only the agreed workflows and parks ERP integration for a separate session. Immediately afterward, Mara reports that the Quality Director remains unconvinced, so a focused 15-minute conversation is scheduled.

## Pricing

The group receives a bounded range with explicit scope assumptions. In a one-to-one conversation, Mara says the CFO will resist the upper end. Instead of discounting immediately, the parties reduce year-one deployment scope and write a path to the preferred scope.

## Pilot

The audit is six weeks away. A 48-hour pilot includes Mara and three procurement operators. The CPO remains sponsor rather than product user.

| Task | Observable completion | Business relevance |
|---|---|---|
| Chase one overdue supplier | Owner and reminder history are visible | Tests workflow accountability |
| Export one audit sample | Required evidence can be reviewed | Tests audit readiness |
| Separate scope for a second factory | Factory boundary is preserved | Tests operating-model fit |

Before the pilot opens, the team writes what happens if it succeeds, the target signature date, and the required security, legal, procurement, and implementation actions.

## Procurement and signature

An editable agreement is sent. Procurement requests a high insurance level; counsel resolves the material issue in a live conversation. The CFO is confirmed as signatory before routing. No production data is loaded before signature.

## Lessons

- Executive access does not replace a working champion.
- The demo is a decision tool, not the beginning of discovery.
- Pilot logins are not evidence; agreed task completion is.
- A successful pilot without a signature path creates delay, not momentum.
- Production work begins only after the required authorization and signature.
