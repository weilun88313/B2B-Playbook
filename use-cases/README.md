## Evidence labels

- **Real:** public or properly anonymized case with a source and date.
- **Curated:** a sourced external pattern summarized by the maintainer.
- **Fictional:** a complete teaching example with invented companies, people, and numbers.

Future cases must state the evidence label at the top. Do not present a composite, model-generated, or anonymized story as a named customer result.
