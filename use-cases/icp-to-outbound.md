# Use case: ICP to outbound

**Evidence:** Fictional worked example  
**Company:** Helion, a fictional supplier due-diligence software company  
**Goal:** Decide whether one account deserves active sales time and create the first relevant outreach

## Situation

Helion sells supplier onboarding and recertification software to manufacturing procurement teams. The team is considering Müller Antriebstechnik, a fictional 1,200-person manufacturer with three plants.

Observable evidence:

- Müller is hiring for Supplier Quality.
- It appears in the directory for an upcoming industrial event.
- A customer audit is scheduled six weeks after the event.
- The current workflow uses spreadsheets and email reminders.
- Procurement Operations is visible; IT Security is not.

## 1. ICP decision

Helion's working ICP is procurement operations leaders or CPOs at discrete manufacturers when an audit, supplier-onboarding deadline, or recertification event exposes the limits of spreadsheets, annual questionnaires, and email follow-up.

| Dimension | Score | Evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Use-case fit | 2 | Supplier onboarding and recertification are active jobs |
| Budget / operating fit | 2 | Three plants and a dedicated procurement function |
| Alternative clarity | 2 | Spreadsheet and email reminders are named |
| Observable trigger | 2 | Customer audit in six weeks |
| Committee visibility | 1 | Procurement Operations is visible; security is unknown |
| **Total** | **9/10** | **T1** |

Default action: one-to-one research, resolve the security seat, and prepare a personalized next step.

## 2. Buying committee

| Seat | Working role | Status | Next discovery action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Economic buyer | CPO or CFO | Unknown | Ask who owns audit exposure and budget |
| Champion | Procurement Operations | Visible | Confirm success definition and internal process |
| Technical buyer | IT Security | Unknown | Ask for the data-residency and SSO owner |
| Blocker | Current questionnaire owner | Unknown | Ask who owns reporting definitions today |

## 3. Positioning

> When multi-plant procurement teams face an audit or supplier-onboarding deadline, Helion helps them make reminder ownership and evidence collection auditable without replacing every procurement system.

This statement names the buyer, trigger, result, and switching boundary. It avoids claiming a complete procurement-suite replacement.

## 4. First outreach

**Subject:** Customer audit after Industrials Connect

> Mara, Müller appears to be expanding Supplier Quality while the customer audit is six weeks after Industrials Connect. Teams in that situation often find that spreadsheet reminder ownership—not questionnaire design—is what breaks first. Is that already solved, or worth comparing for 20 minutes before the event?

The message uses only observed or explicitly framed evidence. It does not pretend to know the recipient's internal metrics.

## 5. Success and stop conditions

Success is a qualified reply that confirms or corrects the workflow, trigger, and responsible seat. An open or click is not success.

Stop active pursuit when a hard disqualifier appears, the trigger is false, or repeated attempts produce no additional evidence. Record the reason instead of silently recycling the account.
