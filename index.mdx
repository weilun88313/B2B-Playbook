A practical field guide for founders and small B2B teams finding customers, explaining their product, and helping people succeed with it.

![Understand your buyers, help them choose, and learn from what happens.](/assets/illustrations/library.webp)

**Last reviewed:** 2026-09-06 · **Reading edit:** 2026-09-12

You might be preparing a customer call, writing an outreach email, or wondering what to do with a disappointing campaign report. Start with that problem. The guides combine explanations, examples, and worksheets you can adapt to your own work.

**Free to read.** You can make private working copies of the templates under the [reuse terms](/copyright).

## A note from the editor

I’m Ivan Xu, the founder of Lensmor. I maintain B2B Playbook to make the everyday work of building and marketing a B2B product easier to think through. Bring a question, try an example, and use what helps in your own situation.

The guides bring together sourced methods, illustrative examples, and reusable working files. Examples are labelled; company-reported results are not independent audits. Adapt the advice to your customers, team, and market.

If a step is confusing or an example feels too far from your work, [tell me where you got stuck](https://github.com/weilun88313/B2B-Playbook/issues/new?template=reader-feedback.yml). Please leave out private customer information.

**Current coverage:** 131 published playbooks · 56 working files · 43 curated tools · 20 reading sources · 9 domain guides

The library is published in English. A separate [Chinese introduction](https://github.com/weilun88313/B2B-Playbook/blob/main/README.zh.md) is available on GitHub.
